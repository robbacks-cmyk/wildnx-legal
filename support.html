WildNX

Support

WildNX is a hunting app for British Columbia — it tracks your trail, shows you the regulations for where you are standing, and lets you share your position with people you choose.

Get help

If something isn't working, looks wrong, or you just have a question, email:

WildNX@hotmail.com

Include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug — it makes it much faster to track down.

What to expect

WildNX is made by one person, working out of Cranbrook, BC. There's no support team and no call centre — just me reading the emails that come in. I'll get back to you, but during hunting season it may take a few days rather than a few hours. Thanks for your patience.
