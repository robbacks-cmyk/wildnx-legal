WildNX

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

WildNX is a hunting app for British Columbia. It records where you have walked, shows you the regulations for where you are standing, and lets you share your position with people you choose. This page explains what the app collects, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

WildNX is built and run by Robert Backs, based in British Columbia, Canada. If anything here is unclear, email WildNX@hotmail.com.

What the app collects

Your account

To use WildNX you create an account with an email address and a password. Your email is stored by our hosting provider so you can sign back in. Your password is never visible to us.

Your profile

You can fill in a profile. Nothing in it is required, and you choose what to enter. It can include:

  • A display name that other hunters can search for
  • Your full name, phone number and address
  • Emergency contacts
  • Medical allergies
  • Hunting licence, PAL and FWID numbers
  • A description of your vehicle
  • A message to be included if you send for help

This information exists so that it is available when it matters — to show a conservation officer, or to include in a message asking for help. It is stored on our hosting provider's servers and on your own phone.

Buddies you connect with can only ever see your display name — never your full name, phone number, address, emergency contacts, medical information, or licence numbers, even after a connection is accepted. If you turn on position sharing for a buddy, they can also see your position, on the terms described below.

Your location

WildNX records your position while you are tracking a hunt. It does this continuously, including when your phone is in your pocket and the screen is off, because a trail with gaps in it is not much use.

Your recorded trails are stored on your phone. They are not uploaded anywhere.

The app also uses your position, without storing it, to show you which management unit you are in and whether you are near a boundary or a closure.

Your step history (Motion & Fitness)

If you allow Motion & Fitness, WildNX reads your phone's step counts for the times you recorded a trail, only to tell walking from slow driving. They stay on your phone, are never uploaded, and are deleted with the trail or your account.

WildNX works without it: if you say no, it tells walking from driving by speed alone. You can change your answer at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Sharing your position with buddies

You can connect with other WildNX users as hunting buddies. Both people have to agree before a connection exists.

Once connected, you can turn on position sharing for that person. While it is on, your position — together with a GPS accuracy figure — is sent to our servers, and that buddy can see it. It is off until you switch it on.

Switching it off stops that buddy seeing your position immediately, including positions shared before you turned it off. The stored positions are not deleted right away — they are removed by the automatic seven-day cleanup described below — but from the moment sharing is off, that buddy can no longer read any of them.

Nobody but a buddy you have specifically enabled sharing for can see your position.

Crash reports

When the app crashes, a report is sent to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, so that the problem can be found and fixed. That report contains the technical details of the failure, your device model, your operating system version and the app version, and a short record of which screens you had open and what the app was doing in the seconds beforehand. It does not contain your name, your email or your position. Sentry's servers record the IP address the report came from, as any web service does. Sentry's session replay and screenshot features are never turned on — nothing about what is on your screen is ever recorded or sent.

What the app does not do

  • There is no advertising in WildNX.
  • Your information is never sold, rented or given to anyone for marketing.
  • There is no analytics tracking of how you use the app.
  • Your recorded trails are never uploaded.

Where your information is stored

Accounts, profiles, buddy connections and shared positions are stored with Supabase, a hosting provider, in its us-west-1 region — servers located in Northern California, United States. This means your information is held outside Canada and is subject to United States law, including lawful access requests by United States authorities. Crash reports are held by Sentry.

Maps are provided by Apple Maps, and weather and storm forecasts come from Open-Meteo, a weather service that does not require an account. When the app draws a map or checks the forecast, those services necessarily receive the area you are looking at, under their own privacy terms — for weather, the app rounds your coordinate to roughly 100 metres before sending it, rather than your exact position.

How long it is kept

Your account and profile are kept until you delete your account.

Positions you have shared with a buddy are deleted automatically, every night, once they are more than seven days old.

Crash reports are kept by Sentry for 30 days and then deleted.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app, under your profile. Deletion removes, on our servers: your profile, your buddy connections and every position you have shared. Your buddies stop seeing you and the connection disappears from their list.

It also clears your phone: every trail you have recorded, every field pin and its photos, and everything saved in the app's settings and caches. What is not cleared is your device's own record that you have seen the app's safety warning, and reference material that is not personal to you — cached copies of the regulations, maps and closures, which are the same for every hunter.

Deletion cannot be undone. If you would rather ask us to do it, email WildNX@hotmail.com from the address on your account.

Children

WildNX is intended for licensed hunters and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and it will be removed.

Your rights

You can see and change everything in your profile from inside the app. You can turn position sharing off at any time, for any buddy, without removing them. You can delete your account and everything attached to it.

If you want a copy of what is held about you, email WildNX@hotmail.com from the address on your account.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top will tell you when. Anything that meaningfully affects your privacy will also be shown in the app.
