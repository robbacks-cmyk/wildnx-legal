WILDNX

BC hunting regs, offline map

WildNX is built by one hunter in Cranbrook, British Columbia. It carries the hunting synopsis for all nine regions of the province, with every management unit mapped. The regulations and the unit boundaries live on the phone, so they are there when the signal is not. It warns you as you approach a management unit boundary, records your trail with the phone in your pocket, and composes an SOS message with your coordinates for you to send.

The regs

The synopsis for all nine BC regions, resolved by management unit — open seasons, bag limits, closures and no-shooting areas for the unit you are standing in, rather than a document to scroll through.

The map

Every management unit in the province, drawn on the map, with a warning as you come up on a boundary — so you know which unit's rules apply before you cross it, not after.

The trail

Record your trail with the phone in your pocket and the screen locked. iOS can interrupt background recording, so the map shows a break wherever that happens instead of drawing a line you never walked.
